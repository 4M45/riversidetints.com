About Us

Welcome to Riverside Tints, Riverside CA's premier destination for professional automotive, commercial, and residential window tinting, paint protection film (PPF), ceramic coating, and auto detailing.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide top-tier window film installations and vehicle protection services that elevate the look, comfort, and longevity of your vehicle or property. With over 10 years of experience, our certified technicians are dedicated to precision, quality, and customer satisfaction.

Why Choose Us?

  • Premium Materials: We use industry-leading carbon and ceramic tint films that offer superior heat rejection and UV protection.
  • Expert Installation: Our highly trained installers utilize micro-edge cutting techniques for a flawless, factory-finish look.
  • Lifetime Warranty: We stand behind our work with a comprehensive lifetime warranty against bubbling, peeling, or fading on most of our premium films.
  • Customer Satisfaction: We pride ourselves on transparent pricing, clear communication, and delivering results that exceed expectations.

Our Services

We specialize in a wide range of services including:

  • Automotive Window Tinting
  • Commercial and Residential Window Tinting
  • Paint Protection Film (PPF / Clear Bra)
  • Ceramic Coatings
  • Auto Detailing

We are deeply committed to the Riverside community and strive to be the most trusted name in automotive styling and protection in Southern California.
