How to Clean Tinted Car Windows Without Damaging the Film

By Riverside Tints | September 11, 2026

You've just invested in a premium car window tinting package. Your car looks incredible, and the interior is remarkably cooler. But a few weeks later, the inside of the glass gets smudged. You grab a bottle of Windex and some paper towels, ready to wipe it down. Stop right there!

Improper cleaning is the number one cause of premature window tint failure. While high-quality ceramic films are incredibly durable, they are still applied to the *inside* of your windows, and harsh chemicals can destroy them. Here is the definitive guide on how to safely clean tinted car windows.

The Golden Rule: NO Ammonia

The most critical rule of cleaning window tint is to never, ever use cleaners that contain ammonia. Ammonia is a harsh chemical found in many household glass cleaners (like traditional blue Windex). Over time, ammonia breaks down the adhesive holding the tint to the glass. It causes the film to dry out, turn brittle, bubble, peel, and famously turn that ugly purple color.

What to use instead: Always look for a glass cleaner that specifically states "Ammonia-Free" or "Tint Safe" on the bottle. Automotive-specific glass cleaners from brands like Meguiar's, Chemical Guys, or Invisible Glass (the tint-safe version) are excellent choices. Alternatively, a simple mixture of warm water and a tiny drop of baby shampoo works perfectly.

Ditch the Paper Towels

Paper towels might seem soft, but they are made of wood pulp. On a microscopic level, they are highly abrasive. Wiping tinted windows with paper towels will eventually cause thousands of micro-scratches on the surface of the film, resulting in a hazy, dull appearance, especially when driving into the sun.

What to use instead: Use high-quality, clean microfiber towels. Microfiber is ultra-soft, highly absorbent, and will not scratch your tint. Keep a dedicated set of microfiber towels just for your interior glass to ensure they don't pick up dirt or grease from other parts of the car.

The Proper Cleaning Technique

Follow these simple steps for a streak-free, safe clean:

  1. Spray the Towel, Not the Glass: Lightly mist your ammonia-free cleaner directly onto the microfiber towel, rather than soaking the window. Spraying the window can cause liquid to run down into the door panel and potentially weaken the tint edges over time.
  2. Wipe Gently: Wipe the glass using smooth, horizontal strokes, then follow up with vertical strokes. Do not press too hard.
  3. Buff Dry: Flip the microfiber towel to a dry side (or grab a fresh, dry towel) and gently buff the glass to remove any remaining streaks.

Wait Before You Clean!

If you just got your windows tinted at Riverside Tints, remember the curing process! Do not clean the inside of your windows for at least 7 to 10 days after installation. The film needs this time to fully adhere and cure to the glass. Cleaning it too early can easily peel the edges.

By using the right products and a gentle touch, your high-quality window tint will look flawless and protect your vehicle for years to come!
