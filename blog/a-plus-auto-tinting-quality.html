Why You Shouldn't Settle for Anything Less Than A Plus Auto Tinting

RT
Riverside Tints
Published: July 15, 2026

We've all seen it. You're sitting at a red light in Riverside, and the car next to you has windows that look like they have a terrible skin disease. The tint is bright purple, completely bubbled, and peeling off the edges of the car window. It looks awful, and it completely ruins the aesthetic of the vehicle.

This is what happens when you hunt for the cheapest price instead of hunting for a plus auto tinting. Cutting corners on window film is a guarantee that you'll be paying to have it stripped and redone in less than two years.

The Danger of Cheap Dyed Films

Those bubbly, purple windows are the result of cheap dyed films. The sun's intense UV rays break down the dye, turning it purple, and the extreme heat melts the low-grade adhesive, causing those massive bubbles. If you want xtreme tinting durability, you cannot use dyed film in California.

You need to demand ceramic technology. Ceramic films contain no dyes to fade and no metal to interfere with signals. They are engineered to withstand the most brutal desert heat without breaking a sweat.

Precision Installation Matters

Even the best film on earth will fail if the installer is inexperienced. Flawless, a plus auto tinting requires a dust-free environment, computer-cut patterns, and years of heat-shrinking experience. When applied correctly, a premium ceramic tint looks like factory glass—no gaps, no dust, and absolutely no peeling.

Don't subject your vehicle to a bad tint job. The cost of stripping old, baked-on adhesive off a car window is expensive and time-consuming. Do it right the first time. Choose a shop that offers true xtreme tinting quality, backed by a lifetime warranty. That is the promise we make at Riverside Tints.
